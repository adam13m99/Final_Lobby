# LobbyBaz — Terms of Use

**Version 2026-08-28**

> Plain language, not legal advice. If LobbyBaz ever takes money or grows past
> friends of friends, have a lawyer read this properly.

## What LobbyBaz is

LobbyBaz lets players inside Iran play Dota 2 together when Valve's
matchmaking is not reachable. One player's PC hosts the match. Our relay
carries game traffic between the players in that room, and nothing else.

LobbyBaz is not made by, endorsed by, or connected to Valve Corporation. Dota 2
is Valve's. You need your own copy of the game.

## What we handle

To put you in a room with other players we keep:

- your username, the name other players see, and your password (stored only as
  a hash — we cannot read it, and **we cannot reset it for you**);
- the MMR you type in, which you tell us and we do not verify;
- which room you are in, and when you were last online;
- the messages you send in lobby chat, room chat and private messages;
- basic connection facts — your address as our relay sees it, and how much
  traffic passed — for as long as they are useful for keeping the service
  running.

**We do not read your game traffic.** It is encrypted between your PC and the
relay, and the relay forwards it without looking inside.

**Chat is not private from staff.** Admins can read what is reported to them
and can see the moderation record. If something must stay private, do not type
it here.

## What we ask of you

- Do not use LobbyBaz to harass, threaten, or abuse other players.
- Do not attack, overload, or attempt to break into the service or other
  players' machines.
- Do not pretend to be somebody else, and do not share an account.
- Your declared MMR should be roughly honest. It is how other players decide
  whether a game will be worth playing.
- Do not use the relay for traffic that is not Dota 2.
- Follow the law where you are.

## Hosting a room

When you host, the match runs on your PC and everybody in the room plays
through it for as long as it lasts. They reach you through our relay, so they
never see your address and you never see theirs.

Hosting uses your upload while the match runs. If your PC goes away the room
gets one minute to see you come back, and closes if you do not.

## What we can do about it

Admins can remove you from a room, mute you, or ban your account. A ban ends
your sessions and empties your seat. Every such action is recorded with who
took it and why, and you may ask for that record.

Hosts control their own rooms: they can kick you, and a kicked player is
barred from that room for a few minutes. That is the host's decision, not
ours.

## What we do not promise

LobbyBaz is provided as it is. We do not promise it will be available, that a
match will connect, or that it will not lose your data. It runs on one server
on a domestic network, and that server has bad days.

We are not responsible for what other players do, for anything you lose while
using it, or for anything that happens to your Dota 2 account. If a court
decides we owe you something anyway, it is limited to what you paid us — which
is nothing.

## Your account

You can stop using LobbyBaz whenever you like. Ask an admin to delete your
account and we will remove it and your messages.

**There is no password recovery.** No email address is collected, so there is
nothing to send a reset link to. If you forget your password, that account is
gone and you make a new one. Write your password down somewhere you will still
have it.

## Changes

If these terms change, you will be asked to accept them again the next time
you sign in. The version you accepted is recorded against your account, with
the date.
