Plain language, not legal advice. If LobbyBaz ever takes money or grows past friends of friends, have a lawyer read this properly.

What LobbyBaz is

LobbyBaz puts players in Iran into the same Dota 2 match when Valve matchmaking is not reachable. One player's PC hosts the game. Our relay carries traffic between the players in that room, and nothing else.

LobbyBaz is not made by, endorsed by, or connected to Valve Corporation. Dota 2 is Valve's. You need your own copy of the game.

What we handle

To put you in a room with other players we keep your username, your display name, a hashed password, the MMR you type in if you type one, and the addresses your client connects from. Room chat is kept only while the room is open.

What we do not do

We do not read your game files, we do not sell anything about you, and we do not have a way to recover a password you forget. There is no reset link because there is no email on file.

Hosting a room

When you host, other players connect to your PC through the relay for the length of the match. Your address is not shown to them. If you close the game, the room closes with it.

Fair play

No cheats, no scripts, no deliberate ruining of other people's games, no using the relay for traffic that is not Dota. Rooms are small and everybody knows who you are. Break this and your account stops working.

Your account

It is yours, and it can be deleted from Settings at any time. MMR can be changed once a week so the filters stay meaningful. Nothing is transferable and nothing is paid for.

Changes

When these terms change the version number changes with them, and everybody is asked again. The current version is always readable from Settings.
